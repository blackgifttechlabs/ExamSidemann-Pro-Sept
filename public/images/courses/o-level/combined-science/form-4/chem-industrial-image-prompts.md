# Industrial Processes: teaching assets

Created using the built-in image_gen tool and the imagegen skill. Each process has its own full-width 1536 × 1024 landscape WebP.

Diagrams are shown first. They and the animations fit within the viewport without sideways scrolling. The lesson includes eight interactive process models. They start paused and provide play/pause, stage selection, previous/next, reset and speed controls. Offscreen or hidden-page animation updates pause. Reduced-motion preferences use static stage snapshots. Equipment sizes, particle sizes and elapsed time are illustrative rather than quantitative simulations.

## Scientific checks

- Correct cathode (negative) and anode (positive) connections; electrons in external wires, ions in electrolyte.
- PbBr₂ model uses two bromide ions per lead ion, gives lead at the cathode and bromine at the anode, and distinguishes molten lead from cooled solid lead.
- Water collection heights maintain H₂:O₂ = 2:1; inverted collectors are open at the immersed ends.
- Copper-anode dissolution and deposition on the iron-nail cathode occur together.
- Haber and Contact reaction insets conserve the numbers of each atom; they illustrate reacting sets, not full feed conversion.
- Haber separates liquid ammonia and recycles N₂/H₂. Contact absorbs SO₃ in concentrated acid before dilution of oleum.
- Document conditions retained: Haber 200 atm, iron, 450–500 °C; Contact 1 atm, V₂O₅, 450–500 °C.
- Hydrogen is a Haber feedstock; ammonia is the product. Lead-bromide lead is molten while hot and solidifies on cooling.
