# Organic Chemistry teaching images

Created with the built-in image_gen tool using the imagegen skill. Six individual landscape images (1536 × 1024) are used in the Organic Chemistry lesson and sized to fit the viewport. Exact displayed formulas are drawn as SVG from atom and bond data inside OrganicChemistry.tsx; the structure models were checked for molecular formulas, carbon/hydrogen/oxygen valence, connected atoms and alkene double bonds.

## Scope and checks

The six groups follow the user's supplied document extract: fuels, combustion, hydrocarbons, biogas, ethanol and global warming. The previous alcohol-focused content and unrelated revision summary were removed from this topic. Learning objectives, activities and named resources are retained.

- Fuel comparisons distinguish useful heat transferred per unit of fuel from true percentage efficiency; no invented universal fuel ranking.
- Complete/incomplete combustion distinguishes CO₂/H₂O from CO and/or soot, with water and possibly CO₂ in incomplete mixtures.
- Structures cover methane, ethane, propane, ethene, propene and ethanol. Hydrocarbon formulae contain only C/H; ethanol has its –OH group.
- Biogas uses anaerobic digestion and suitable pH/temperature; gas leaves the dome towards the burner.
- Ethanol fermentation uses the document's 30–35 °C range. Malt breaks starch into fermentable sugars, and yeast ferments sugars. Ordinary fractional distillation enriches ethanol rather than making water-free ethanol.
- Climate material links combustion and deforestation with CO₂ and a stronger greenhouse effect.
