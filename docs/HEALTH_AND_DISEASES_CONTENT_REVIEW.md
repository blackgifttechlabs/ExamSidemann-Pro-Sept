# Health and Diseases content review

Scope: the topics supplied by the user. No new diagram requirements were introduced. Teaching content uses concise explanations, comparison tables, life-cycle steps and the specified activities. Treatment descriptions are general educational summaries, without doses or self-treatment instructions.

Coverage: physical/mental/social wellbeing; personal and food hygiene; burial/recycling/burning; disease causes and transmission; bilharzia; gonorrhoea/syphilis/chancroid/genital herpes; malaria/typhoid/Ebola/cholera; malaria life cycle and mosquito control; tobacco/alcohol/mandrax/cannabis/solvents; immunity and infant immunisation; HIV/AIDS.

Accuracy notes:
- Freshwater snails are intermediate hosts for bilharzia; larvae penetrate skin. Infection is not restricted to stagnant water.
- Bacterial and viral STIs are distinguished; herpes antivirals control symptoms rather than eliminating infection.
- Malaria is caused by Plasmodium, not by the mosquito itself.
- Mandrax is a sedative; hallucinations are not treated as the defining effect of every drug.
- Immunisation schedules are referred to current national guidance instead of an invented fixed timetable.
- HIV is distinguished from AIDS; routine contact does not transmit HIV.
